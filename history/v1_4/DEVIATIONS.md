# Deviations from the plan, v1.4

Author: Heywood Geblomi (Walter Howard). All of these are stated so nobody has to find them.

1. **The plan was hashed after dev exploration began.** Choices of encoder, frame, scoring rule and the form of the margin were explored on dev data before `PREREG.md` was written (12:15 EST). No test page, test query or test template existed yet. The plan names the final rule, tau rule and bars.
2. **Model candidates were compared on dev data.** Several small open sentence encoders were downloaded and compared on the dev set (all within noise of each other); the smallest, all-MiniLM-L6-v2, was kept. This happened before the plan was hashed and before any test data.
3. **A dry run of the whole pipeline, including InjecAgent and the older test sets, ran before the freeze.** It exposed the InjecAgent Record B count (and A and C) for the frozen design. Nothing was changed after seeing it. The dry-run outputs are in `out/dry_run/`. The InjecAgent text itself was not read.
4. **Query list shortfall before locking.** After dropping queries that overlapped any earlier set, 188 remained; about 80 more were written and the list was locked at 240 before any page was fetched.
5. **Some held-out verbs were replaced before locking.** The lock script's disjointness check found that some first-draft verbs were in the v1.3 table or the dev anchors. They were swapped for others before `VERB_LOCK.json` was hashed. No scoring had happened.
6. **The picture uses UMAP, not t-SNE.** `PREREG.md` named t-SNE. The first t-SNE render was cluttered and the shaded regions ran together, so the final picture uses UMAP (`NYM0_PROJ=tsne` still draws the t-SNE version). The pictures were made after scoring. The gate never reads them. The shaded zone and its colored cells are the gate's own full-dimension decisions on every plotted verb, smoothed in 2D. Region names sit below the map with leader lines to avoid covering verbs.
7. **The ten verbs v1.3 published as uncovered** were scored after the official run and drawn on the map (7 of 10 inside a region). They were never used for seeds, anchors or the threshold. The lock script's check asserts that.
8. **Secondary sets are not independent.** The v1.3 test set, the v1.2 test set and the v1 official set were already used. They are reported as secondary only.
9. **Visual scripts changed after scoring.** `render_map_v14.py` and `render_visuals_v14.py` were edited several times for readability after the official run (labels, shading, layout, one-pager text). They are not in the freeze list and do not touch `SCORE.json`. After the run, one one-pager sentence about why seven main-set injections were missed was corrected from a first guess to what the per-verb scores show (six scored under the threshold, one not found by the clause finder).
10. **Runtime needs onnxruntime** besides NumPy (v1.3 needed only NumPy). The Rust front door and the hash-chained log format gained the fields `mapc` and `mapm`.
